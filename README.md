## 🌟 Project Overview
**REWIRED: The Prefrontal Recovery Club** is an engaging, empathetic, and scientifically grounded word-matching and cognitive rehabilitation board game for **2 to 4 players**. 

Designed to destigmatize behavioral recovery from digital dopamine loops (such as compulsive pornography consumption and late-night doomscrolling), the game places players in the shoes of members in a supportive weekly gathering. Through **active vocabulary expansion, semantic matching, and verbal sentence challenges**, players systematically exercise their prefrontal cortex, clear their "brain fog," and race toward 100% Neuroplastic Mastery.

## 🎭 The Story & Premise
In a world saturated with hyper-stimulating digital novelties, a group of self-aware individuals notice the subtle toll on their everyday cognitive faculties: tip-of-the-tongue word retrieval struggles (*anomia*), scattered attention, and mental lethargy.

Instead of hiding in isolation or self-blame, they form **The Prefrontal Recovery Club**—a weekly tabletop gathering where members challenge each other to cognitive workouts, celebrate healthy lifestyle habits, laugh at relatable digital traps, and support one another in rewiring their brains back to peak clarity.

## 📈 The 5 Stages of Brain Recovery (0% – 100%)
Each player has their own dual-layer **Brain Progression Tracker**. As players advance from 0% to 100%, they unlock enhanced cognitive capabilities (higher hand limits, defensive shield slots, and combo scoring):

| Stage | Progress | Cognitive State | In-Game Perk / Ability |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1** | **0% – 20%** | **The Dopamine Fog** | Baseline hand limit: 3 cards. Lethargic verbal retrieval. |
| **Stage 2** | **21% – 40%** | **Synaptic Spark** | Hand limit expands to 4 cards. Flickers of executive clarity. |
| **Stage 3** | **41% – 60%** | **Prefrontal Rebound** | Gain 1 free **Urge Surfing Token**; unlock ability to store up to 2 shield tokens! |

| **Stage 5** | **81% – 100%** | **Neuroplastic Mastery** | Transcendent focus and eloquence. **The first player to reach 100% wins!** |

## 🕹️ How the Gameplay Works

Each turn is fast, engaging, and contains **zero player downtime**:

```
[Phase 1: Draw & Habit Check]  ---> Draw up to hand limit. Resolve any drawn Life Event.
           |
           v
[Phase 2: Forge a Neuro-Link]   ---> Match a Word Card to its Meaning / Synonym.
           |
           v
[Phase 3: Verification & Speech] ---> Check Synaptic ID. Deliver an original spoken sentence.
                                     Gain +10% (or +15% for Dual Synonyms)!
```

### 1. The Neuro-Link (Word + Meaning/Synonym Matching)
Players can match a Word Card from their hand or the central display with a Clue Card (Definition, Synonyms, or Fill-in-the-Blank Context). 

### 2. Error-Free Verification (The Synaptic ID)
Every Word Card and its corresponding Clue Card share an identical discreet **Synaptic ID** (e.g., `#04`). No disputes, no ambiguity—players instantly flip to verify!

### 3. The Verbal Fluency Challenge (Broca's Area Activation)
To earn their +10% progression, the active player must construct an original, creative sentence out loud incorporating the vocabulary word. The table (the "Support Circle") listens and validates the sentence with a nod or table-knock.

### 4. Support Circle Assist
Stuck in the fog? Ask an ally for a 1-word hint. If successful, **both players gain +5% progression**, fostering true camaraderie.

## ⚡ The Habit Loops & Triggers (Life Events)
Based on the behavioral science of *Cue $\rightarrow$ Craving $\rightarrow$ Routine $\rightarrow$ Reward*, players encounter realistic real-life events:

- 🛑 **Trigger / Relapse Setbacks (-5% or -10%):**
  - *"The 1:15 AM Bedroom Loop"* (Alone in bed with a phone)
  - *"The Boredom Vacuum"* (Unstructured Sunday afternoon with open tabs)
  - *Safety Rule:* A setback can never drop you below the floor of your current 20% Stage!
- 🌟 **Keystone Healthy Habits (+5%, +10%, or +15%):**
  - *"Sunday Mud Football"* (Aerobic exercise releasing BDNF)
  - *"Offline Cooking"* (Mindful sensory engagement)
  - *"Cold Shower & Wim Hof Breathing"* (Sympathetic reset)
- 🛡️ **Urge Surfing Shields:**
  - Players can spend an **Urge Surfing Token** to completely neutralize a relapse trigger and earn +5% bonus progression for active prefrontal impulse control!

## 🔬 Scientific Research & Literature
The gameplay systems are grounded directly in empirical peer-reviewed neurobiology:
1. **Kühn & Gallinat (2014), *JAMA Psychiatry*:** Structural MRI findings showing striatal adaptation and weakened functional connectivity between the prefrontal cortex (DLPFC) and striatum in compulsive digital consumption.
2. **Laier, Schulte, & Brand (2013), *Journal of Sex Research*:** Empirical proof of working memory and executive control degradation following exposure to supernormal appetitive sexual cues.
3. **Brand et al. (2016, 2019), *Addictive Behaviors* & *Frontiers in Psychiatry*:** The I-PACE model (Interaction of Person-Affect-Cognition-Execution) illustrating how executive function training restores self-control.
4. **Diamond (2013) & Ardila (2010):** Cognitive neuroscience demonstrating that verbal fluency tasks, semantic search, and lexical assembly stimulate neuroplasticity and build cognitive reserve.
