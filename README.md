# REWIRED: The Prefrontal Recovery Club
### A Vocabulary-Building Neuroplasticity Board Game (2–4 Players)

> **"Your prefrontal cortex is the CEO of your mind. It's time to take back the boardroom."**

---

## 🧠 Project Overview
**REWIRED: The Prefrontal Recovery Club** is an engaging, empathetic, and scientifically grounded word-matching and cognitive rehabilitation board game designed for 2 to 4 players. 

Players step into the shoes of participants in a supportive weekly gathering, dedicated to rewiring their brains from digital dopamine addiction (problematic pornography consumption and compulsive screen loops) back to optimal cognitive sharpness, focus, and verbal eloquence.

By exercising **verbal fluency, lexical retrieval, and executive inhibition**, players advance their personal **Brain Progression Tracker (0% to 100%)**. Along the way, players navigate real-life habit triggers, build keystone routines, deploy "Urge Surfing" shields, and leverage mutual accountability to achieve 100% Neuroplastic Mastery.

## 🎮 Game Specifications
- **Players:** 2 – 4 Players (Competitive, Team, or Full Co-Op Mode)
- **Ages:** 14+
- **Play Time:** 30 – 45 Minutes
- **Core Mechanics:** Lexical Matching, Synonym Linking, Verbal Fluency Sentence Challenges, Habit Loop Events, Prefrontal Shielding.
- **Tone:** Uplifting, witty, compassionate, non-judgmental, strictly respectful, and 100% free of degrading tropes.
